About the Game

Since its global launch on December 12, 2018, on iOS and Android, it has captivated tens of millions of players worldwide with its perfect blend of fast-paced action, strategic depth, and ever-evolving seasonal content.

The game masterfully fuses elements of hero shooters, MOBAs, battle royales, and team-based arena fighters into bite-sized matches that typically last between two to three minutes, making it ideal for quick sessions during commutes, breaks, or late-night grinding.

Every Brawler is a larger-than-life, cartoonish yet surprisingly expressive character bursting with personality. Precise controls let you maneuver maps, hide in bushes, and turn the tide with custom supers.

Official Game Modes

💎

Gem Grab

Collect 10 crystals from the mine and keep them secure. Countdown starts immediately. Kill the crystal carriers to disrupt the timer!

🌵

Showdown

Battle royale rules. Open crates to collect green power cubes. Avoid the creeping poison fog cloud closing in on the borders.

⚽

Brawl Ball

Soccer mode. Run into the ball to carry it, and shoot attacks to kick the ball. Score twice in the opponent's net to win.

💰

Heist

Protect your safe while trying to crack open the enemy team's safe. Coordinate assaults and defenses with your team.
